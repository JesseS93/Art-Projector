Marker strip

The app uses these three squares to pin your picture to the paper, so it stays in place when the paper or phone moves.

Back to the app
  1. Print this page. Any size works, so "fit to page" is fine. Keep the squares sharp and black.
  2. Cut along the dashed line and tape the strip flat just above your drawing area, or print it straight onto the top of your drawing paper.
  3. Keep the strip in the camera view and don't cover it with your hand while you trace. If one or two squares get covered, the app keeps working.

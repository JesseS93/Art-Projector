TraceLens

See your picture on your paper through the camera, then trace it.

  1. Print the marker strip and tape it across the top of your paper.
  2. Prop your phone 20–30 cm above the paper, pointing down, so the camera sees the strip and your drawing area.
  3. Choose a picture, then move, resize and rotate it with your fingers.
  4. Tap Lock. The picture is now pinned to the paper and follows it if the paper or phone moves. Pinch to zoom in on detail.

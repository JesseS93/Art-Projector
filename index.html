TraceLens

See your picture on your paper through the camera, then trace it.

  1. Prop your phone 20–30 cm above the paper, camera pointing down. A glass or a small stand works.
  2. Choose a picture, then move, resize and rotate it with your fingers.
  3. Tap Lock. Then pinch to zoom in on detail. The picture stays pinned to the paper.
